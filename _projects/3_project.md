### Deformable active chiral shells: intrinsic chirality induced counter-rotation and necking

Left-right symmetry breaking during developmental morphogenesis of many organs and organisms is associated with chiral processes, which are driven by active chiral components.

We develop a generic theoretical framework of active chiral shells which can capture the activities induced by chiral motors embedded in a thin shell, such as cell cortex and epithelial tubes. In analogy to micropolar elasticity, the active matter consisting of chiral shells contains both the translational and spin degrees of freedom, and the activity includes active stress and active couple stress. We find both geometric-driven and chemical-driven chiral processes in a deformable chiral fluid shell, and highlight the role of active drilling and active bending induced by active torque dipoles in curved geometry.
