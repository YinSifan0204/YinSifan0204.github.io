From February 2027 I will be a CNRS Chargé de Recherche at LAI, [CENTURI](https://centuri-livingsystems.org/), on the Luminy campus in Marseille, where I am starting the theory group **Active matter morph-YIN Group**. I am recruiting **postdocs and PhD students to start in Fall 2027**.

Join a new theory group in CENTURI, Marseille to study how active forces, chemical signals and growth shape cells and tissues — the **biomechanics of developmental morphogenesis in active matter**.

### Research

- 3D morphodynamics of active shells
- Chiral active matter
- Active nematic solids
- Cell–ECM interactions in tissue morphodynamics
- Theory and simulation, in close collaboration with experimentalists

More on past and ongoing work: [projects](/projects/) and [publications](/publications/).

### We are looking for

- A master's degree in mechanics, applied mathematics, applied physics or a related field
- Background in continuum mechanics, nonlinear dynamics or computational science
- Enthusiasm for analytical theory, numerics and living systems

### How to apply

Send your CV, transcripts and a short statement of research interests to **syin@pks.mpg.de**.
