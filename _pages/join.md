From February 2027 I will be a CNRS Chargé de Recherche at LAI, [CENTURI](https://centuri-livingsystems.org/), on the Luminy campus in Marseille, where I am starting the theory group **Active matter morph-YIN**. I am recruiting **PhD students to start in Fall 2027**.

We will study how active forces, chemical signals and growth shape cells and tissues, using analytical theory and numerical simulation in close collaboration with experimentalists.

### Research directions

- 3D morphodynamics of active shells
- Chiral active matter theory
- Cell–ECM interactions in tissue morphodynamics
- Theory and simulation, in close collaboration with experimentalists

More on past and ongoing work: [projects](/projects/) and [publications](/publications/).

### Who we are looking for

- A master's degree in mechanics, applied mathematics, applied physics or a related field
- Background in continuum mechanics, nonlinear dynamics or statistical physics
- Enthusiasm for analytical theory, numerics and living systems

### How to apply

Send your CV, transcripts and a short statement of research interests to **syin@pks.mpg.de**.
